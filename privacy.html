Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ai Factory is an internal content tool we use to publish our own short videos to our own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok). This policy explains what data the tool handles.

What we access

When an account owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok shares with us, with the owner's permission: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and the right to publish videos the owner chooses (scope video.publish). We also read the posting options TikTok returns for the account (allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length).

How we use it

Only to show the owner which account they are posting to and to upload the video, caption and settings the owner selects on the posting page. Nothing is posted without the owner pressing “Post”. We do not read followers, messages or other users' data, and we do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored on our own server, readable only by the tool, and never shared with third parties. Videos are our own rendered content.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data. It is sent only to the platform the owner chose to publish to.

Deletion

Disconnect at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, or email us and we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.
